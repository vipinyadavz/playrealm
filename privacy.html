PlayRealm

Privacy

PlayRealm has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. Nothing you do in a game is sent anywhere.

What is kept, and where

Everything PlayRealm remembers is saved in your own browser, on your own device, using the browser's local storage:

  • your best scores;
  • your profile: level, XP, badges, day streak, daily quests and play time;
  • your favorites and the games you played recently;
  • your settings (sound, full screen, on-screen controls);
  • the scoreboard and player names of a game night in progress.

We cannot see any of it. It is not synced, so a different browser or device starts fresh.

How to erase it

  • Reset profile, at the bottom of the Profile window, clears your level, badges, favorites and history.
  • Clear best scores, at the bottom of the home page, clears every saved score.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything at once, including the offline copy.

Offline copy

So that the games keep working without a connection, your browser stores a copy of the site's own files (pages, scripts, fonts). That copy holds nothing about you.

Other servers

  • Hosting. Like every website, the company that hosts PlayRealm receives your IP address and basic browser details in order to deliver the pages, and may keep short-lived technical logs. We do not add any tracking of our own.
  • Word Bomb. When you open the game Word Bomb while online, it downloads a public English word list from the jsDelivr content network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which therefore sees your IP address. The game still works, with a smaller word list, if that download is blocked or you are offline. No other game contacts another server.

Fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.

Children

PlayRealm collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
