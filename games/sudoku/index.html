Sudoku

Fill the grid so each row, column and box has 1 to 9 exactly once.

The twist: Completing a row, column or box earns a hint, so clean solving pays off.

Play Sudoku All 57 games

How to play

Every row, column and 3×3 box must hold the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Pick a cell and enter a digit; switch on notes to pencil in candidates. A wrong digit is a strike, and three strikes end the puzzle. The twist: each row, column or box you finish correctly flashes and charges one hint (up to 3). A hint reveals the selected cell, or the most useful cell if none is open. Units finished by a hint do not charge another, and every hint you never use adds 150 points.

Controls

  • Click↑↓←→ Select a cell (WASD works too)
  • 1–9 Enter a digit (Shift+digit for a note)
  • NSpace Toggle notes mode
  • Backspace0 Clear the cell
  • H Use a hint
  • Z Undo

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
