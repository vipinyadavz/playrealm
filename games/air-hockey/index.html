Air Hockey

Slam the puck into the other goal on a frictionless table.

The twist: Comeback goals: every goal you score makes your own goal wider.

Play Air Hockey All 57 games

How to play

Push your mallet around your half and knock the puck into the other goal. Hit it while moving to send it faster. Your action key gives a short dash; the ring around your mallet shows when it is ready. Every goal you score widens your own goal by 20%, so a lead is never safe. First to 7 wins. Against the computer you can also drag your mallet with the mouse or a finger.

Controls

  • WASD Coral: move
  • Space Coral: dash
  • ↑←↓→ Cyan: move
  • Enter Cyan: dash
  • Drag Move your mallet (vs computer)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
