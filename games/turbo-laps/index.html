Turbo Laps

Race three laps of a twisty top-down track against four rivals.

The twist: A ghost of your best lap races beside you, and beating it fills your nitro.

Play Turbo Laps All 57 games

How to play

Race three laps against four computer drivers. Hold forward to drive, back to brake or reverse, and steer with left and right; grass slows you down and gold pads give a quick boost. Your first lap sets a ghost car. From then on the ghost replays your best lap right beside you: cross the line ahead of it to fill your nitro bar and set a new ghost. Hold your action key to burn nitro on a straight. Tuck in behind another car to slipstream past it. With 2 players the screen splits and each player chases their own ghost.

Controls

  • WASD Coral: drive and steer
  • Space Coral: nitro
  • ↑←↓→ Cyan: drive and steer
  • Enter Cyan: nitro
  • Hold Drive toward the pointer (1 player)
  • Tap bar Nitro burst (1 player)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
