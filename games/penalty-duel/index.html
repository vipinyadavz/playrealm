Penalty Duel

Take turns shooting and saving in a five-kick penalty shootout.

The twist: Keepers can fake a dive to bait the kicker, but only once per round.

Play Penalty Duel All 57 games

How to play

Each side takes five penalties, then it goes to sudden death. As the kicker, stop the moving target where you want the ball to go, then stop the power meter: green is good, red sails over the bar. Hold left or right during the run-up and flight to curve the shot. As the keeper, dive with your direction keys once the ball is struck (add up for a high dive); the sooner you react, the further you reach, but dive before the kick and the kicker can bend it away. The twist: before the kick each keeper may fake once per round. You lean one way where the kicker can see it, and a dive the other way gets a burst of speed. Roles swap every kick.

Controls

  • Space Coral kicker: stop aim, then power
  • AD Coral: curve the shot, or fake and dive as keeper
  • WS Coral keeper: high dive / stay big
  • Enter Cyan kicker: stop aim, then power
  • ←→↑↓ Cyan: curve, or fake and dive as keeper
  • Tap vs computer: tap to stop aim and power, or tap where to dive

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
