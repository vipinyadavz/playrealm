Block Climb

Race three rivals up a block tower while the lava rises below.

The twist: You can carry only 8 blocks: break blocks below you to place them above.

Play Block Climb All 57 games

How to play

Climb to the flag at the top before your three rivals, and stay ahead of the lava rising from below. Run and jump between ledges; when a wall is too tall, build. Hold click (or F) to mine a nearby block and right-click (or E) to place one; press E in mid-air to drop a block under your feet and climb a pillar. The twist: your bag holds only 8 blocks, so recycle the blocks below you to keep building up. Grab coins, and press G to punch a rival off a ledge. Score: height × 10, coins × 25, plus a bonus for your finish place.

Controls

  • AD Run
  • WSpace Jump
  • Hold clickF Mine a block (S+F takes one from below you)
  • Right-clickE Place a block (E in mid-air: under you)
  • G Punch a rival

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
