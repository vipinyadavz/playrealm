Outpost Defense

Fortify a lonely outpost by day and survive creature waves by night.

The twist: Build turrets by day, then hold the walls at night with only a flashlight cone.

Play Outpost Defense All 57 games

How to play

Protect the glowing core in the middle of your outpost. By day, click the slots on the walls to build a gun turret, slow tower or flame post, click a built one to upgrade it, and press R to repair walls. Press Enter to start the night early. At night the creatures come from every edge: fast mud crawlers, spitting stilt walkers and brutes that smash walls. The twist: it is pitch dark. Your flashlight cone and the small turret lamps are the only light, and anything outside it is just a pair of glowing eyes. Score 1000 per night survived plus 10 per kill.

Controls

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Aim the flashlight and rifle
  • ClickSpace Shoot
  • Click slot Build or upgrade (day)
  • 123 Pick a tower in the build menu
  • R Repair walls (day)
  • Enter Start the night early

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
