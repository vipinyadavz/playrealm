Mine Field

Clear the field using number clues without setting off a mine.

The twist: Big openings charge a scanner, and one shield forgives a mistake.

Play Mine Field All 57 games

How to play

Each number tells you how many of the eight cells around it hide a mine. Reveal every safe cell to win. Your first click is always safe. Any reveal that opens 10 or more cells at once charges the scanner (up to 3): scan a cell to reveal its 3×3 area safely and auto-flag the mines inside. You also carry one shield: the first mine you step on breaks it instead of ending the run. Faster clears score more, and an unused shield adds 15%.

Controls

  • Click Reveal (click a satisfied number to open its neighbors)
  • Right-clickF Flag
  • Hold Flag on a touch screen
  • S Scan, then pick a cell
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor
  • SpaceEnter Reveal at the cursor

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
