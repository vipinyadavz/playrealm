Pool

Top-down 8-ball: clear your solids or stripes, then sink the black.

The twist: Sink the gold ball and you shoot again, no matter what.

Play Pool All 57 games

How to play

Break the rack, then claim solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15) with your first legal pot after the break. Pot one of your balls to keep shooting. Fouls hand your opponent ball in hand (place the cue ball anywhere): potting the cue ball, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a cushion after contact. Clear your group, then sink the 8 to win; sinking it early or with a foul loses. The twist: a neutral gold ball sits near the top rail. Hitting it first is never a foul, and potting it gives you another shot no matter what else happened. It comes back once per rack.
