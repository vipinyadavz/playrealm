Snowball Siege

A first-person snowball free-for-all against five snow-folk.

The twist: Pack a snow wall in front of you in a second; snowballs chip walls away.

Play Snowball Siege All 57 games

How to play

Three minutes, six snow-folk, one icy arena. Every snowball that lands scores a point, and the third hit knocks a player out for 3 seconds for 3 more points. Snowballs fly in a gentle arc, so lead moving targets. The twist: press E to pack a snow wall in the cell in front of you. It blocks throws, but every snowball chips it, and after 3 hits it crumbles. You can keep 3 walls at once; packing a fourth melts your oldest.

Controls

  • WS Move
  • AD Strafe
  • Mouse Look (click the view to lock)
  • ←→ Turn without a mouse
  • ClickSpace Throw a snowball
  • ERight-click Pack a snow wall

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
