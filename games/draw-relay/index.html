How to play

For 3 to 8 people sharing one device. Everyone starts a chain by writing a prompt (or tapping Give me one). Then the chains travel around the group: the next player sees only the last step, so they draw the words they read, and the player after them describes the drawing they see. A cover screen hides everything while you pass the device. When every chain has one step from each player, gather round for the reveal and watch each prompt mutate, step by step, from the first words to the final result. No letters or numbers in drawings!

Controls

  • MouseTouch Draw on the paper
  • 1–8 Pick an ink color
  • X Change brush size
  • E Toggle the eraser
  • Z Undo the last stroke
  • Enter Submit a prompt or description
  • →Space Next step in the reveal
  • ← Previous step in the reveal

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
