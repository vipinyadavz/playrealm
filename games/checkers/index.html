How to play

Move one piece diagonally forward to an empty dark square. Jump an enemy piece by hopping over it into the empty square behind; jumping is mandatory, and you keep jumping with the same piece while you can (glowing pieces must capture). Reach the far row to crown a king, which moves and jumps both ways. Win by taking every enemy piece or leaving your opponent with no move. Forty moves in a row without a capture is a draw. The twist is Rush mode: each turn has a 10-second fuse, and if it burns out you lose a random piece and your turn. The computer plays under the same fuse.

Controls
