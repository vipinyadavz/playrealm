How to play

Sink the ball in the cup in as few strokes as you can across nine holes with sand, water, hills, sliding gates, a spinning bar, bumpers and a tunnel. Drag back from the ball and let go to putt; the dotted line shows where it rolls first. Water costs a penalty stroke and puts you back where you hit from, and a hole ends at 8 strokes. The twist: before every hole you pick a ball. Bouncy rolls far and gets big kicks off bumpers, sticky stops fast and grips hills, heavy shoves sliding gates and spinners aside and ignores bumpers. You can use each type only 3 times per round, so plan where each one helps most. Up to 4 players take turns on each hole.

Controls

  • Drag Pull back from the ball, release to putt
  • ←→ Aim
  • ↑↓ Power
  • Space Putt (Enter works too)
  • ADWS Aim and power, same as arrows
