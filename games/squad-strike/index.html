Squad Strike

A top-down 4v4 team shootout where no cover lasts for long.

The twist: Every wall can be shot down, so cover never lasts.

Play Squad Strike All 57 games

How to play

Coral team (you, a friend and bots) against the cyan bots. First team to 25 knockouts wins, or the team ahead after 4 minutes. Grab the SMG, shotgun and rifle lying on the floor, and throw grenades to blast groups. The twist: every lavender brick wall cracks and falls when it is shot, so hiding only buys time. Only the dark riveted pillars last. Health comes back if you stay out of fire for a few seconds.

Controls

  • WASD Move (P1)
  • MouseClick Aim and shoot (1 player)
  • Space Shoot where you face (P1, 2 players)
  • G Grenade (P1)
  • R Reload (P1)
  • ↑↓←→ Move (P2)
  • Enter Shoot (P2)
  • . Grenade (P2)
  • / Reload (P2)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
