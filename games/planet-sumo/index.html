Planet Sumo

Chunky sumo wrestlers shove each other around a tiny spinning planet.

The twist: Shove your rival off a tiny planet whose gravity keeps changing strength.

Play Planet Sumo All 57 games

How to play

Two sumo wrestlers share a tiny planet. Left and right walk you around it (relative to your own feet), up jumps. Tap your action key to shove: a quick lunge that knocks your rival back, with a 0.8 s cooldown. Hold it for 0.6 s until you glow gold, then let go for a charged shove that hits much harder. Every 8 seconds gravity shifts, with a 2-second warning ring on the planet: LOW is floaty, so shoves fling people off; HEAVY is sticky and slow. Now and then a reverse pulse pushes everyone outward. Hold down to crouch: you get heavier, take half the knockback and stay anchored, but move slowly. Drift past the faint escape ring and your rival scores. First to 5 wins.

Controls

  • AD Player 1 walk around the planet
  • WS Player 1 jump / hold to crouch
  • Space Player 1 shove (hold 0.6 s to charge)
  • ←→ Player 2 walk
  • ↑↓ Player 2 jump / hold to crouch
  • Enter Player 2 shove (hold to charge)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
