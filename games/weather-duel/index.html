Weather Duel

Rain and shine over a shared garden to bloom more flowers than your rival.

The twist: Steer a weather spirit over a shared garden: flowers bloom for whoever gives them the right mix of rain and sun.

Play Weather Duel All 57 games

How to play

You are a weather spirit floating over a garden of 40 seeds. Your action key switches you between a rain cloud and a sun, and whatever is under you gets watered or warmed. Each seed shows two pips, a drop and a sun, and their size says how much of each it needs. Whoever fills the last missing bit makes it bloom in their color, so you can finish a seed your rival started. Flowers can be stolen back: rival rain floods them and rival sun scorches them until they wilt into seeds again (your own weather soothes them). A flooded seed stays soaked and only needs sun; a scorched one only needs rain. Grab a wind gust, then double-tap your action key to dash and blow your rival away. A rare rainbow makes your next 3 seeds bloom on touch. Most flowers after 75 seconds wins the round; best of 3.

Controls

  • WASD Player 1 float
  • Space Player 1 switch rain / sun (double-tap: gust)
  • ↑↓←→ Player 2 float
  • Enter Player 2 switch rain / sun (double-tap: gust)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
