Snake

Eat, grow longer, and never bite your own tail.

The twist: Every 8 bites your tail breaks off and turns to stone, and bites score more.

Play Snake All 57 games

How to play

Steer the snake to the glowing food. Each bite makes you longer and a little faster. On every 8th bite you molt: the back half of your body breaks off and becomes stone walls, and every bite after that is worth one more point. Your tail flashes the bite before it happens, so park it somewhere harmless. Gold food is worth 5× your multiplier but vanishes fast. Hitting a wall, stone or your own body ends the run. Pick Wrap mode to pass through the edges instead.

Controls

  • ↑↓←→ Steer
  • WASD Steer
  • Swipe Steer on a touch screen

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
