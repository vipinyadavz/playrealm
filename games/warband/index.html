Warband

Gather gold, hire soldiers, archers and knights, and outlast four rival lords.

The twist: Your army follows your banner in formation: switch between wedge, ring and line on the fly.

Play Warband All 57 games

How to play

Ride your lord around the map to scoop up coins and stand in gold mines to capture them; mines pay out every second. Spend gold to hire soldiers (10), archers (15) and knights (30), up to 40. They march behind your banner and fight any enemy that comes close. The twist: press Q to switch formation. Wedge hits 60% harder while you gallop, ring shields your army and you, and line makes archers fire long-range volleys. Defeat a rival lord and their army scatters. If your lord falls, the game ends. Score is gold earned, plus 10 per kill and 500 per lord defeated.

Controls

  • WASD Ride
  • MouseTouch Hold to ride toward the pointer
  • Q Switch formation
  • 123 Hire soldier, archer, knight

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
