Bumper Blitz

Six karts, one arena, and a crate of weapons on every corner.

The twist: Drift to charge your weapon: the longer you slide, the bigger the blast.

Play Bumper Blitz All 57 games

How to play

Drive over a crate to grab a rocket, mine, shockwave, shield or boost, then fire it at the other karts. Every kart has 3 hit points; knock one out to score a KO. It respawns 2 seconds later. The twist: hold the drift key while turning to slide. The ring around your kart fills through tiers 1, 2 and 3, and the next weapon you fire is upgraded to that tier: triple rockets, wider blasts, longer shields. A charge lasts 3 seconds after the slide ends. Oil slicks make you slide for free; zap pads flash yellow before they shock. First to 10 KOs or most KOs after 3 minutes wins.

Controls

  • WASD P1 drive
  • QL-Shift P1 drift (hold while turning)
  • Space P1 fire
  • ↑↓←→ P2 drive
  • /R-Shift P2 drift
  • Enter P2 fire

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
