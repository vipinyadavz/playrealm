Flag Ball

Roll into the rival base, steal their flag and haul it home before they bump you.

The twist: The flag carrier gets heavier and slower the longer they hold the flag.

Play Flag Ball All 57 games

How to play

Coral and cyan teams of three rolling balls. Touch the enemy flag in their base to grab it, then roll it back to your own base while your flag is home to score. Any bump from an enemy pops the carrier and sends the flag back. Dash into an enemy to daze them for a second. Spikes pop anyone for 3 seconds. Speed pads fling you along their arrows, and a ball sitting on the top-lane button opens the center gate for its team only. The twist: the flag is heavy. The gold ring around a carrier fills as they hold it, and the fuller it gets the slower they roll and the less pads and dashes help. First to 3 captures, or the most after 5 minutes, wins.

Controls

  • WASD P1 roll
  • Space P1 dash (dazes enemies you hit)
  • ↑↓←→ P2 roll
  • Enter P2 dash (dazes enemies you hit)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
