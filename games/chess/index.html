Play Chess All 57 games

How to play

Checkmate the enemy king: attack it so it has no safe move. All the usual rules apply: castling, en passant, promotion (pick the new piece), stalemate, the 50-move rule, threefold repetition and insufficient material. Play the computer at three levels or a friend on this screen. The twist is fog mode for two players: you see only your own pieces and the squares they can attack or move to. There is no check in the fog, so you may move into danger, and capturing the enemy king wins. Pass the device when the cover asks, so your opponent cannot peek.
