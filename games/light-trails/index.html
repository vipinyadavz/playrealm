Ride a light cycle and box your rival in.

The twist: Hop over any wall once every 5 seconds, and old trails fade away.

Play Light Trails All 57 games

How to play

Each rider leaves a wall of light behind it. Hit a wall, any trail or the edge and you lose the round. Press your action key to hop 3 cells forward over anything; you must land on an empty cell, and the hop recharges in 5 seconds (the ring around your rider fills up). Trails fade after 7 seconds, so dim light is about to vanish, and the riders slowly speed up. First to 3 rounds wins.

Controls

  • WASD Player 1 steer
  • Space Player 1 hop
  • ↑↓←→ Player 2 steer
  • Enter Player 2 hop

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
