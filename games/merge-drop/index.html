Merge Drop

Drop cosmic pieces into the jar and merge matching ones into bigger worlds.

The twist: Every 15 merges a comet falls and smashes the smallest pieces in its path.

Play Merge Drop All 57 games

How to play

Move the dropper and release a piece into the jar. When two pieces of the same kind touch they merge into the next one up: dust, pebble, ice chip, moonlet, moon, ring world, gas giant, sun, blue star, nova and galaxy. Each merge scores the new piece’s value, and two galaxies make a supercluster bonus. If any piece sits above the dashed line for 2 seconds the jar overflows and the run ends. The twist: every 15 merges a comet lines up for a second and a half. Steer its path with the dropper, then it streaks through the jar and smashes every dust and pebble it touches, scoring more for each one in a row.

Controls

  • MouseTouch Aim, click or release to drop
  • ←→ Move the dropper (A / D too)
  • Space Drop (Enter or ↓ too)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
