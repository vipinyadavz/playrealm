Pixel Logic

Use number clues to fill in the grid and uncover a hidden pixel picture.

The twist: Each solved puzzle becomes one tile of a bigger mosaic picture.

Play Pixel Logic All 57 games

How to play

The numbers beside each row and above each column list the runs of filled cells in that line, in order. "3 1" means a run of three, at least one gap, then a single cell. Fill cells to match every clue, and mark cells you know are empty with an ×. Drag to paint a whole line at once. A clue dims when its line matches. The twist: the 16 pictures are tiles of one big beach scene. Each one you solve drops into the mosaic in color, and you can pick any tile from the gallery. Turn on Check if you want wrong fills caught and counted.

Controls

  • ClickDrag Fill cells (drag paints a line)
  • Right-clickHold Mark a cell as empty
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor (WASD works too)
  • SpaceEnter Fill at the cursor (hold and move to paint)
  • X Mark at the cursor (hold and move to paint)
  • ZBackspace Undo / clear a cell
  • CGR Check on/off, gallery, clear puzzle

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
