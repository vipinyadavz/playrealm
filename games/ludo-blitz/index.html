Ludo Blitz

Race your four tokens around the cross and home before everyone else.

The twist: Power tiles on the track hand out shields, double moves and swaps.

Play Ludo Blitz All 57 games

How to play

Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto your start square, then race clockwise around the board and up your colored home column. A 6 gives you another roll, but three 6s in a row lose the turn. Land on a rival to send it back to its yard, except on star and start squares, which are safe. You need the exact roll to reach home. The twist: six glowing power tiles. Shield protects that token for your next two turns, ×2 doubles your next roll, and Swap trades places with the nearest rival token up to 12 squares ahead. First home wins; play goes on for 2nd and 3rd.
