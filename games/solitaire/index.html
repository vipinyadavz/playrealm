Solitaire

Classic patience: sort the whole deck onto four suit piles.

The twist: One wild card per game can stand in for any card you need.

Play Solitaire All 57 games

How to play

Build the four foundations up by suit from ace to king. On the seven tableau piles, stack cards down in alternating colours; only a king (or a run starting with one) can fill an empty pile. Click the stock to turn cards (one or three at a time). Drag cards or runs, click a card to send it to its best spot, double-click to send it home. The twist: once per game, press Use wild and drop the wild on any tableau card or foundation. It becomes exactly the card that fits there and plays as that card. When the real card turns up (in the waste, or face up on top of a pile), it takes the wild's place and the wild is gone. Using it costs 25 points.

Controls

  • Drag Move a card or a run
  • Click Send a card to its best spot
  • Double-click Send a card to a foundation
  • ←→↑↓ Choose a pile
  • Enter Pick up, then drop (twice on the same pile: best spot)
  • Space Turn the stock
  • W Use the wild card
  • Z Undo
