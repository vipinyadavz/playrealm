Hex Claim

Paint the hex map in your color, one daring loop at a time.

The twist: Leave your land to draw a trail, loop back to claim everything inside, but anyone who crosses your trail knocks you out.

Play Hex Claim All 57 games

How to play

You start with a small patch of hexes and never stop moving. Step off your land and you leave a trail behind you. Get back onto your land and the trail, plus everything it encircles, becomes yours, even land other players owned. The catch: while you are out, your trail is your weak spot. If anyone touches it, you are knocked out, and if you touch your own trail, so are you. Cross a rival's trail to knock them out instead. Gold hexes give a 3 second speed boost. Your best score is the biggest share of the map you held. Own 60% to win outright.

Controls

  • WASD Steer (8 directions)
  • ↑↓←→ Steer (8 directions)
  • MouseTouch Steer toward the pointer

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
