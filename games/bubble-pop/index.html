Bubble Pop

Aim, bounce and pop bubbles in groups of three or more.

The twist: The ceiling only drops when you miss, and bomb bubbles clear a whole ring.

Play Bubble Pop All 57 games

How to play

Shoot bubbles at the cluster hanging from the ceiling. Land next to two or more of the same colour to pop them, and anything left hanging without a link to the ceiling falls for bonus points. Bank shots off the side walls; the guide shows your first bounce. The twist: only misses move the ceiling. A shot that pops nothing lights a pip, and four pips drop the ceiling one row, while shots that pop never move it. Every 12 shots a bomb joins the queue and blasts every bubble within two rings of where it lands, whatever the colour. Clear the board to reach the next level; if bubbles cross the line at the bottom, the game ends.
