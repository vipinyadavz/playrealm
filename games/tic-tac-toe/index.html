Tic-Tac-Toe

Three in a row wins, on a board that never fills up.

The twist: You only get three marks: placing a fourth erases your oldest.

Play Tic-Tac-Toe All 57 games

How to play

Line up three of your marks to win. Each player keeps at most three marks on the board: when you place a fourth, your oldest one (shown faded and cracked, with a 1) is erased first, and the new mark cannot go into the cell it just left. The board never fills, so a round with no line after 30 moves is a draw. The starting player alternates each round.

Controls

  • Click Place a mark
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor
  • EnterSpace Place at the cursor
  • 1–9 Place by number pad layout (7 is top left)
