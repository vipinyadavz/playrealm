Wall Rush

Ten players drop onto an island. Loot, build, and be the last one standing.

The twist: Drop an instant wall to soak up bullets, while the storm closes in every 40 seconds.

Play Wall Rush All 57 games

How to play

You and nine CPU players start with an axe. Find pistols, SMGs, shotguns and rifles in houses and crates, grab ammo and medkits, and chop trees or crates for wood. The twist: press Q to drop a wooden wall right in front of you. It costs 10 wood, takes 120 damage before it breaks, and shows cracks as it weakens. Every 40 seconds the storm shrinks toward the white circle on the minimap, and it hurts more each time. Score 100 points per place above last, plus 150 per elimination.

Controls

  • WASD Move
  • MouseClick Aim and shoot
  • ↑↓←→Space Aim and shoot without a mouse
  • QRight-click Drop a wall (10 wood)
  • E Pick up
  • R Reload
  • 123 Switch slot (empty slot = axe)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
