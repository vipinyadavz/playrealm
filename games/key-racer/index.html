Key Racer

Type a short passage as fast as you can to race three rival cars.

The twist: Typos make your car skid, and clean streaks charge a nitro burst.

Play Key Racer All 57 games

How to play

Type the highlighted word and press Space to move on. Every correct letter pushes your car toward the finish line, while three rivals type at their own pace. A typo makes your car skid to a stop until you fix it with Backspace. Type 15 words in a row without a mistake and nitro fires on its own: each of your next 5 words pushes your car 3 extra letters. Your score is your typing speed in words per minute.

Controls

  • A–Z Type the highlighted word
  • Space Finish the word
  • Backspace Fix a typo and stop skidding

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
