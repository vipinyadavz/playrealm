How to play

Coral and Cyan are tied together by a stretchy rope. Each blob rolls, hops (weakly) and can GRIP: hold the grip key while touching any wall, floor, ceiling, lift or windmill blade and that blob sticks fast. The other blob then swings from it like a pendulum: push the way you are swinging to pump it higher (press away from a wall you bump to kick off it), grip something new, and swap roles hand over hand. Let go of a grip mid-swing to fling. While gripping, hold jump to reel your pal in; a pal right next to a gripping pal hops off them for a big boost, so you can inchworm up walls. Water is safe but slow, lifts and windmill blades carry whoever grips them. Spikes and falls send you both back to the last flag (R does it on purpose). Get both blobs into the gate. Your time is the total for all 8 courses. Solo? Play both blobs at once: left hand drives Coral, right hand drives Cyan.

Controls

  • AD Coral: roll
  • W Coral: hop / boost off a gripping pal (while gripping: reel in)
  • Space Coral: hold to grip
  • ←→ Cyan: roll
  • ↑ Cyan: hop / boost off a gripping pal (while gripping: reel in)
  • Enter Cyan: hold to grip
  • R Back to last flag

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
