Bounce the ball, break every brick, catch the power-ups.

The twist: The ball takes the color of the last brick it hits, and same-color bricks shatter in one hit.

How to play

Move the paddle to keep the ball in play and break every colored brick; steel never breaks. The ball glows with the color of the last brick it touched. Hitting a brick of that same color shatters it at once, however tough, and grows a chain multiplier (×2, ×3 …). A different color only chips 1 HP and recolors the ball, resetting the chain. Catch falling capsules for power-ups, but dodge the grey Shrink. You have 3 lives.

Controls

  • ←→ Move paddle
  • AD Move paddle
  • MouseDrag Move paddle
  • SpaceClick Launch / release a caught ball

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
