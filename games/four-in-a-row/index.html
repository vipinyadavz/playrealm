Four in a Row

Drop discs and connect four before your rival does.

The twist: Each player gets one bomb disc that blasts its neighbors out of the grid.

Play Four in a Row All 57 games

How to play

Take turns dropping discs into the seven columns. Four of your color in a row, column or diagonal wins. Once per round you can arm your bomb (press B or tap the Bomb button): it drops like a disc, then blows up, removing itself and the discs directly left, right and below it. Discs above fall into the gaps. If the blast gives both players four, the bomber wins. A full grid is a draw.

Controls

  • ←→ Pick a column
  • EnterSpace↓ Drop
  • 1–7 Drop in that column
  • B Arm or disarm your bomb
  • Click Drop, or tap Bomb under your tray

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
