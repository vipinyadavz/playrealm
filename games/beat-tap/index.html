Beat Tap

Tap four lanes in time with a song built live from drums, bass, chords and lead.

The twist: Every instrument lives in its own lane: miss too many and that part of the song drops out.

How to play

Notes fall down four lanes. Tap D, F, J or K (or touch the lane) as each note crosses the line; long notes on Normal and Hard must be held to the end. Timing earns Perfect, Great or Good, and every 10 hits in a row raise your multiplier. Each lane is one instrument: drums, bass, chords and lead. Misses drain that lane, and when it empties the instrument goes silent and the song sounds thinner. Hit 8 notes in a row in a silent lane to bring it back. If all four drop out, the song stops. Use Calibrate if taps feel early or late.

Controls

  • DFJK Tap or hold the four lanes
  • Tap Touch a lane
  • +− Nudge the audio offset (in menus)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
