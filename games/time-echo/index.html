Time Echo

Collect orbs while dodging ghosts of your own past moves.

The twist: Every 6 seconds a ghost spawns and replays exactly what you just did.

Play Time Echo All 57 games

How to play

Move the glowing dot to collect gold orbs, one at a time. Every 6 seconds an echo appears where you were 6 seconds ago and retraces your path, then walks it back and forth forever. New echoes are harmless while they fade in; after that, touching one ends the run. Grab orbs quickly for a bonus, skim past echoes for close-call points, and spend a Rewind (earned every 3 orbs, hold up to 2) to erase your oldest echo.

Controls

  • ↑↓←→ Move
  • WASD Move
  • Space Rewind: erase the oldest echo
  • Hold Move toward the pointer; tap the round button to rewind

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
