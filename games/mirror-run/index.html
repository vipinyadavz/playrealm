Mirror Run

Guide two linked runners through mirrored worlds onto their flags.

The twist: Steer two runners at once: the one below lives upside down and moves the opposite way.

Play Mirror Run All 57 games

How to play

One set of controls moves two runners. The coral runner walks the top world; the cyan runner hangs upside down in the world below and always moves the opposite way, and both jump together. Get both onto their flags at the same time to clear a level. Push a runner into a wall to make it wait while the other keeps walking: that is how you get them in step. Spikes and pits rewind the level, crumbling tiles give way, and a switch flips the doors of its colour in the other world. Each level scores 100 plus a time bonus for beating par. 15 levels; new ones unlock as you clear them.

Controls

  • ←→ Move (the bottom runner goes the other way)
  • AD Move
  • SpaceW↑ Jump (both runners, hold for higher)
  • R Rewind to the level start
  • M Level menu

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
