Hoop Shot

Sink as many baskets as you can in 60 seconds.

The twist: The hoop slides faster with every streak, and swishes count double.

Play Hoop Shot All 57 games

How to play

Flick up from the ball to throw it at the hoop, or aim with the arrow keys and hold Space to charge. Each basket is worth 2 points, and a swish that touches neither rim nor board counts double. Baskets in a row build a streak that raises your multiplier, but the hoop starts sliding along its track and gets faster with every make. Miss once and the streak resets and the hoop slows to a stop. The last 10 seconds are heat time: every point doubles. Your first few throws show the arc. Play solo for a best score, or take turns against a friend or the computer.

Controls

  • Flick Drag up from the ball and release to throw
  • ←→ Aim (A and D work too)
  • Space Hold to charge, release to throw (Enter works too)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
