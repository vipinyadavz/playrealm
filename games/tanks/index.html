How to play

Drive your tank through a new maze each round and hit the other tank with a shell. Shells bounce off walls, so you can shoot around corners, but your own shells can hit you too. Gold and violet walls take turns: only one color is solid, the other is a dashed ghost you can drive and shoot through. They swap every 6 seconds, and the incoming color flickers for a second first. Grab crates for a spread shot, fast shells or a shield. First to 5 wins.

Controls

  • WS Coral: drive
  • AD Coral: turn
  • Space Coral: fire
  • ↑↓ Cyan: drive
  • ←→ Cyan: turn
  • Enter Cyan: fire

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
