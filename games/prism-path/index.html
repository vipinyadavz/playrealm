Prism Path

Bend, split, filter and mix beams of light to wake every crystal.

The twist: Split white light with prisms and mix colors to wake crystals that answer to only one color.

Play Prism Path All 57 games

How to play

A source shoots a white beam across the board. Drag pieces from the tray onto empty cells to steer it. Mirrors bounce light; click one to turn it. Prisms split white into red (straight on), green and blue (to the sides); colored light passes through them untouched, and a click swaps the sides. Filters keep only their own color. Mergers add up every beam that enters them (red + green = yellow, red + blue = magenta, green + blue = cyan, all three = white) and fire the mix out of their arrow; click to aim. Each crystal wakes only for its exact color, and any other color hitting it keeps it dark. Light every crystal at once to clear the puzzle. 20 puzzles, 100 points each, plus 25 for every tray piece you did not need.

Controls

  • Click Turn / flip / aim a piece
  • Right-clickHold Return a piece to the tray
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor
  • 1234 Pick a tray piece
  • Space Place / turn at the cursor
  • Backspace Remove at the cursor
  • Z Undo
  • R Clear the board

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
