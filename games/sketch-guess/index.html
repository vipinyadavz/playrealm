How to play

For 2 to 8 people around one screen. On your turn, only you look while you pick one of three secret words: easy, medium or hard. Then everyone watches you draw and shouts guesses. The word shows as blanks, and a letter appears at half time and again at three quarters. When someone gets it, tap their name: the faster the guess, the more points they get (up to 100), and you get 50 for each correct guesser. The twist: your ink tank drains as you draw (thick lines drink twice as fast) and undo or clear never give it back. Each hint letter refills 30%.

Controls

  • MouseTouch Draw on the paper
  • 123 Pick the easy, medium or hard word
  • 1–6 Pick an ink color while drawing
  • X Thin or thick brush
  • Z Undo the last stroke (no ink back)
  • Hold P Peek at your word
  • Enter Start drawing

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
