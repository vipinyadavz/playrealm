Switch Up

Match suit, number or action and be the first to empty your hand.

The twist: Play a 7 to swap your whole hand with another player.

Play Switch Up All 57 games

How to play

Everyone starts with 7 cards. On your turn, play one card that matches the top card by suit (color and shape), number or action, or play a Rainbow and pick the next suit. Can't play? Draw one; if it fits you may play it. Skip jumps the next player, Flip reverses the direction (with two players it works like Skip), +2 and +4 Rainbow make the next player draw and lose their turn. The twist: any 7 lets you swap your whole hand with a player of your choice. Going down to one card? Press Last card! before your turn ends, or draw 2 if someone calls you out first. Catch a player who forgot for the same penalty. First to empty their hand wins the round.

Controls

  • ←→ Select a card
  • EnterSpace Play the selected card
  • D Draw a card (or keep the drawn card)
  • L Last card! (or catch someone who forgot)
  • 1–4 Pick a suit after a Rainbow
  • MouseTouch Tap a card, the pile or a button

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
