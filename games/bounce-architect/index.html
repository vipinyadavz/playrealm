Bounce Architect

Draw quick bounce lines to guide a falling ball through rings and into the cup.

The twist: You can't touch the ball: draw short-lived bounce lines to guide it through every ring.

Play Bounce Architect All 57 games

How to play

A ball drops from the launcher at the top and you can never touch it. Instead, press, drag and release to draw a straight bounce line; the ball rebounds off it at whatever angle you drew. Lines fade after 3 seconds, only 3 can exist at once (the oldest vanishes), and they cost ink, which refills slowly. Pass the ball through every gold ring to unlock the cup, then drop it in. Bumpers fire it off, coral spikes pop it, wind pushes it and portals warp it. You have 3 balls per level. A dotted guide shows the first bounce while you draw. Endless drop is a bottomless shaft: collect rings and dive deep.

Controls

  • Drag Draw a bounce line
  • ↑↓←→ Move the crosshair
  • Space Start / finish a line at the crosshair

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
