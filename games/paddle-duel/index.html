Paddle Duel

Classic two-paddle tennis, first to 11.

The twist: Rally heat: long rallies set the ball on fire and make the point worth up to 3.

Play Paddle Duel All 57 games

How to play

Move your paddle to return the ball. Where it hits your paddle sets the angle, and a moving paddle puts a slight curve on it. The ball speeds up with every hit. After 6 hits in a rally it turns gold and the point is worth 2; after 12 it blazes and is worth 3. Tap your action key just before the ball reaches you to smash it back faster. First to 11 wins.

Controls

  • WS Coral: move
  • Space Coral: smash (tap just before contact)
  • ↑↓ Cyan: move
  • Enter Cyan: smash

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
