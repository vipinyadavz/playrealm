Dojo Duel

A tight one-on-one fighter where perfect timing beats big damage.

The twist: Block at the last instant to parry and leave your rival wide open.

Play Dojo Duel All 57 games

How to play

Knock your rival’s health to zero or have more left when the 60-second timer runs out. Best of 3 rounds. Light attacks are quick, heavies are slow but knock down, and light, light, heavy chains into a combo. Standing lights fly over a crouching rival; crouching heavy sweeps the legs. Double-tap a direction to dash. Hitting and getting hit fill your meter; at half a bar you can fire a lunging special. Hold block to guard. The twist: tap block in the last moment before a hit lands (within about a tenth of a second) to parry. You take nothing, and the attacker is stunned for over half a second, wide open for your best combo.

Controls

  • AD P1 walk (double-tap to dash)
  • WS P1 jump / crouch
  • FG P1 light / heavy
  • Space P1 block (tap late to parry)
  • R P1 special
  • ←→ P2 walk (double-tap to dash)
  • ↑↓ P2 jump / crouch
  • ./ P2 light / heavy
  • Enter P2 block (tap late to parry)
  • Right Shift P2 special

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
