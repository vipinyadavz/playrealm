Number Slide

Slide the board and merge matching numbers into bigger ones.

The twist: Every 12 moves a frozen tile drops in, and only a merge right beside it thaws it.

Play Number Slide All 57 games

How to play

Slide every tile at once with the arrow keys, WASD or a swipe. Two equal numbers that bump together merge into their sum, and the merged value is added to your score. A new 2 or 4 appears after each move. The twist: every 12 moves a block of ice lands on the frosted cell. Ice never slides, so tiles stop against it like a wall. Each merge in a cell right next to the ice cracks it, and two cracks thaw it into a free small tile. Reach 2048 to win, then keep going if you like. You get 3 undos per game, each costing 50 points.

Controls

  • ↑↓←→ Slide
  • WASD Slide
  • Swipe Slide on a touch screen
  • ZBackspace Undo one move (−50 points, 3 per game)
