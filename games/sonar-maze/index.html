Sonar Maze

Find the exit of a pitch-dark maze using sound.

The twist: Your sonar pings reveal the walls, but the stalkers hear them too.

Play Sonar Maze All 57 games

How to play

The maze is dark: you only see a small glow around yourself. Press Space to send a sonar ping that lights up nearby walls for a few seconds. You get 5 pings per level, plus any you find. Stalkers are blind, but every ping tells them where you were, and they rush to that spot. You only see them when a ping or your glow touches them. Reach the gold exit (it pulses every 3 seconds). Getting caught costs a life and sends you back to the start.

Controls

  • ↑↓←→ Move (hold two to queue a turn)
  • WASD Move
  • Space Sonar ping
  • Tap Tap beside yourself to move that way; round button pings

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
