Gem Swap

Swap neighbouring gems to line up three or more of a kind.

The twist: Gravity turns every 10 moves, so gems start falling sideways.

How to play

Swap two neighbouring gems to make a line of three or more of the same kind. Matched gems shatter, the rest fall and new ones drop in, and every chain reaction raises the combo multiplier. A line of four makes a line gem that clears its whole row or column, an L or T makes a burst gem that clears a 3×3 block, and a line of five makes a prism: swap it with any gem to clear every gem of that kind. The twist: every 10 moves gravity turns a quarter clockwise (down, left, up, right), so gems slide sideways and new ones pour in from a different edge. The compass warns you two moves ahead. Moves mode gives you 30 swaps; Timed mode gives you 90 seconds.

Controls

  • DragSwipe Swap a gem with its neighbour
  • ClickClick Select a gem, then a neighbour
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor
  • SpaceEnter Pick up a gem, then press an arrow to swap

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
