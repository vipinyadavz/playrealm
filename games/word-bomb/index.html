How to play

The bomb shows a chunk of letters like ING, TR or OU. Type any real word that contains it (3 letters or more, never one already used this game) and press Enter. Solo endless: your time bank drains every second; each word adds 2 seconds plus half a second for every letter beyond 4, so long words keep you alive while the chunks get harder. Your score is the total letters typed. Pass the bomb: 2 to 4 players share the keyboard, a valid word passes the bomb on, and a hidden fuse (7 to 15 seconds) ticks faster and faster. Words of 8+ letters push the fuse back 2 seconds. Whoever holds it when it blows loses a life; the last player standing wins.

Controls

  • A–Z Type a word
  • Enter Submit the word
  • Tab Skip the chunk in solo (costs 3 s)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
