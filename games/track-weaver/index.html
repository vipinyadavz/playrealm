Track Weaver

Run a busy little railway and route every colored train home without a crash.

The twist: Flip track switches live to send every colored train to its matching station.

Play Track Weaver All 57 games

How to play

Trains roll out of the tunnels at the edge of the map and never stop. Every junction has a numbered switch, and its gold arrow shows where the next train will go. Click or tap a junction, or press its number, to flip it, but you cannot flip it while a train is on top of it. Send each train to the station of its own color: longer and faster trains (white stripe = express) score more. A wrong station or a crash is a strike, and three strikes end the run. Trains that slip into a tunnel come back later. A marker at a tunnel mouth shows the next train two seconds early. New colors open as the run goes on, and a good score on a map unlocks the next one this session.

Controls

  • ClickTap Flip the junction under the pointer
  • 1–9 Flip the junction with that number

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
