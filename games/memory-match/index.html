Memory Match

Flip cards two at a time and find every pair.

The twist: Every miss makes two hidden cards swap places, so watch closely.

Play Memory Match All 57 games

How to play

Flip two cards per turn. Matching pictures stay face up. When you miss, both flip back and two hidden cards slide across the table to swap places (two swaps on Hard), so follow them with your eyes. Three matches in a row earn a peek that shows every hidden card for a second. Streaks score extra, misses cost a little, and a quick finish adds a time bonus.

Controls

  • Click Flip a card
  • ↑↓←→ Move the cursor
  • EnterSpace Flip the card at the cursor
  • P Use a peek

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
