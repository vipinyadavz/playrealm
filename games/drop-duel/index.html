Drop Duel

Stack three-cell gems, clear lines and bury your rival in garbage.

The twist: Clears fire garbage rows at your rival; dig through garbage to send it back doubled.

Play Drop Duel All 57 games

How to play

Two boards, eight wide and sixteen tall, fed the same three-cell pieces: a straight 3 and a corner. Fill a row to clear it. Clearing 2 lines at once sends 1 garbage row to your rival, 3 lines send 2. Garbage rows are grey stone with one gap, and they rise from the bottom after a short warning on the meter beside the board. The twist: clearing a garbage row is a dig, and digs send double, so a buried board can fight back hard. Clears also cancel garbage that is still waiting on your meter. The pieces fall faster every 30 seconds, and after a minute and a half both boards get a garbage row every few seconds. Top out and you lose the round. Best of 3.

Controls

  • AD P1 move
  • W P1 rotate (E turns the other way)
  • S P1 soft drop
  • Space P1 hard drop
  • Q P1 hold
  • ←→ P2 move
  • ↑ P2 rotate (Period turns the other way)
  • ↓ P2 soft drop
  • Enter P2 hard drop
  • / P2 hold
