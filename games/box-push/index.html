Box Push

Push every crate onto a pad without boxing yourself in.

The twist: Ice tiles make boxes slide until they hit something.

Play Box Push All 57 games

How to play

Walk around the warehouse and push every crate onto a gold pad. You can only push, one crate at a time, and never pull, so a crate in a corner is stuck for good. The twist: a crate pushed onto ice keeps sliding until it hits a wall or another crate, or glides onto plain floor. You walk on ice normally. Pads on ice only hold a crate that something stops there. There are 20 levels; each one cleared is worth 100 points, plus 50 if you finish within par (the fewest possible moves). Undo as often as you like.

Controls

  • ↑↓←→ Walk and push (WASD works too)
  • TapSwipe Step or walk to a tapped spot
  • ZBackspace Undo a move
  • R Restart the level
  • L Level select

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
