Bounce from platform to platform and climb as high as you can.

The twist: Spend 5 coins to drop a rescue cloud under your feet when you fall.

Play Sky Hopper All 57 games

How to play

You bounce automatically whenever you land on a platform; steer left and right to pick where you land. Leaving one side of the screen brings you back on the other. Blue platforms move, cracked gold ones break after one bounce, springs launch you high and pale clouds fade in and out. Grab a jetpack for a 3 second boost. Above 1500 m, spiky floaters knock you down. Collect coins: when a fall looks fatal, spend 5 to drop a rescue cloud right under your feet. Score is your best height in meters plus 5 per coin collected.

Controls

  • ←→ Steer
  • AD Steer
  • Hold left / right Steer on a touch screen
  • SpaceCloud button Rescue cloud (5 coins)

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
