Tide Keeper

Raise and lower the sea to bring boats home and put out fires.

The twist: You control the sea level: float boats home and drown fires, but keep the villages dry.

Play Tide Keeper All 57 games

How to play

You only control the sea level. Each boat flies a colored, numbered sail: when it reaches the cliffs, bring the sea to the dock with the same flag and hold it there a moment to moor it. Higher docks pay more; hold a boat at the wrong dock until its coral ring fills and it moors there for a penalty. Fires catch on the ledges and slowly spread: sink one under the sea for 1 second to douse it. But a stilt village floods if the water stays above its floor for 2 seconds, and a fire that grows on a village ledge burns it. Each costs one of your 3 hearts. Collect floating tide locks to seal the villages for 6 seconds, raise the sea for a visiting whale so it spouts on the fires, and brace for storms.

Controls

  • ↑↓ Raise / lower the sea
  • WS Raise / lower the sea
  • Drag Pull the tide lever (or drag anywhere)
  • Space Use a tide lock

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
