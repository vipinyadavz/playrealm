How to play

You are a magnet disc. Your magnet always pulls the steel ball toward you, so you can grab it and carry it. Hold your action key to flip the magnet to push: the ball blasts straight out from your centre, and a rival close by gets shoved too. Pushing drains your meter (the ring around your disc), which refills while you pull. Line the ball up between you and the goal, then flip. First to 5 goals wins.

Controls

  • WASD Player 1 move
  • Space Player 1 hold to push
  • ↑↓←→ Player 2 move
  • Enter Player 2 hold to push

Works with a keyboard, a controller, or on-screen controls on a phone. Free, no sign-up, and it keeps working offline.
